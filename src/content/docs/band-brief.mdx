## Why this app exists

We're a progressive band, and we aren't satisfied with how our songs compare to Tool, King Crimson, System of a Down, Pantera and Meshuggah. The gap isn't the playing or the material. It's **construction**. Tool is the band we rate highest, and much of the beauty of their songs comes from their form. We have played their music for years and know its nuances and textures, but we have never studied it as architecture.

The aim isn't to sound like Tool. We want to understand how great forms work deeply enough to design **our own formal frameworks**: unconventional (not pop, jazz or Classical templates), but strong enough that complex material feels inevitable. They should be carried by our own textures and material.

<KeyIdea>Our material is strong. We're building the architecture it deserves.</KeyIdea>

## How we write

- Often the drummer writes a part and the band develops it. Sometimes Ali brings riffs and melodies.
- Recently Ali and the drummer design **most of the forms**, often working remotely, and the others create material to fit those formal patterns.
- We share ideas as Guitar Pro and MIDI files.

That split matters to this app. Form design is a separate job, done by two people, often at a distance. So the app needs to help with designing a form *before* the material exists, and with handing a form plan to the rest of the band.

## Working rules

- Steal the principle, never the surface.
- Complexity has to be *felt* before it's disrupted.
- Every concept gets tested on a real EP track.
- The app is shared with the band soon, and everything in it is meant to be shared.
- Our own transcriptions of reference tracks may go in the motif bank for private study. Recordings and published tabs don't.
