## Gaps no verified source fills

The Tool lab and our own analysis have to cover these.

- Song-level analyses of *Schism*, *The Grudge*, *The Patient*
- Form in System of a Down and Pantera
- Multi-movement form in prog metal; post-metal form
- The breakdown as a formal function
- Konnakol and Carnatic tala (the drummer's world-rhythm side beyond Hindustani tala)
- Any 2025–26 research on meter in metal
- **Psychology:** no study uses prog or metal, odd-meter groove, or music longer than about 6 minutes. Chills haven't been studied in prog or extreme metal. Juslin's emotion work and the classic chill studies still need checking.
